Why this place matters

A building full of history—and future.

For decades, 28–32 Ellingfort Road was home to Hackney Chinese Community Services: a place for connection, support and belonging.

It can serve the community again. Once a public building is sold to private developers, getting it back is nearly impossible. We’re asking Hackney Council to stop the sale and work with local people to return it to community use.
